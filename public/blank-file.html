Who is this for?+

First-years writing a Java exam or a supplementary exam this season, who follow the lectures but freeze on a blank file. If you've never written a line of Java, the pace will be fast, but the daily commits will keep you moving.

My university's Java module is different. Will this help?+

The 4 weeks cover the core of first-year Java, from variables to classes and objects. If your module tests something extra, ask in Discord and I'll point you to the right practice.

I'm writing exams right now. Do I have time?+

A daily commit can be 20 minutes. Live lessons are recorded, so you can watch them when your timetable allows. The point is small, daily practice, not extra hours.
